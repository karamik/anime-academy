# 30 Ego Spirits

Every weapon in the game is possessed by a spirit — a character with a personality, a voice, and quirks. They comment on the fight, joke, complain, and react to events.

**Distribution:** 12 R (common), 10 SR (rare), 6 SSR (epic), 2 UR (legendary).

Each spirit speaks in 4 situations:
- `onEquip` — when equipped
- `onKill` — after a kill
- `onLowHP` — low health
- `onBoss` — during a boss fight

---

## R — Common (12)

### 1. Bokken "Woodstick"
- **Type:** Melee
- **Personality:** Tired veteran. Complains that "weapons were heavier in his day".
- **Line:** *"Swinging me like a stick again. I'm an ancient artifact, you know."*
- **Quirk:** Constantly grumbles about modern youth.

### 2. Dagger "Whisper"
- **Type:** Melee
- **Personality:** Paranoid. Always whispers that "someone's behind you".
- **Line:** *"Quiet... do you hear that? They're close. They're ALWAYS close."*
- **Quirk:** Afraid of the dark, despite being an assassin's weapon.

### 3. Sling "Pebble"
- **Type:** Ranged
- **Personality:** A child. Naive, enthusiastic. Celebrates every hit.
- **Line:** *"Yay! I hit! I HIT IT!"*
- **Quirk:** Asks to be "petted" after battle.

### 4. Spear "Dryloaf"
- **Type:** Melee
- **Personality:** Boring bureaucrat. Speaks only in facts and numbers.
- **Line:** *"Hit probability: 67%. Recommend revising tactics."*
- **Quirk:** Keeps statistics on every strike.

### 5. Bow "String"
- **Type:** Ranged
- **Personality:** Poet. Speaks in verse, even mid-combat.
- **Line:** *"Arrow flies — the enemy falls. Beauty in every motion."*
- **Quirk:** Writes haiku after each kill.

### 6. Hammer "Basher"
- **Type:** Melee
- **Personality:** Good-natured idiot. Doesn't understand complex words.
- **Line:** *"BAM! Good! More BAM?"*
- **Quirk:** Can only count to three.

### 7. Scythe "Reaper"
- **Type:** Melee
- **Personality:** Grim philosopher. Contemplates death.
- **Line:** *"We all die. The only question is — who first."*
- **Quirk:** Collects enemies' "last words".

### 8. Pistol "Click"
- **Type:** Ranged
- **Personality:** Nervous. Stutters. Afraid of gunshots.
- **Line:** *"I-I-I don't w-want to... FINE, SHOOTING!"*
- **Quirk:** Apologizes to every enemy killed.

### 9. Shield "Wall"
- **Type:** Support
- **Personality:** Silent defender. Speaks in single words.
- **Line:** *"Behind. Me."*
- **Quirk:** Never attacks, only protects.

### 10. Whip "Lash"
- **Type:** Melee
- **Personality:** Sadist. Enjoys the enemy's pain.
- **Line:** *"More! MORE! They scream so deliciously!"*
- **Quirk:** Counts strikes out loud.

### 11. Fan "Lady"
- **Type:** Support
- **Personality:** Aristocrat. Despises "commoners".
- **Line:** *"Don't touch me with your dirty hands, peasant."*
- **Quirk:** Refuses to work if the player is badly dressed.

### 12. Slingshot "Titch"
- **Type:** Ranged
- **Personality:** Bully. Teases everyone.
- **Line:** *"Ha! Missed? You suck."*
- **Quirk:** Throws rocks even at allies.

## SR — Rare (10)

### 13. Rasetsu — Segmented Fang
- **Type:** Melee (whip-sword)
- **Personality:** Aggressive, cocky. Rushes into battle.
- **Line:** *"Wake up, they're about to eat us! GET UP!"*
- **Quirk:** Urges the player with shouts.

### 14. Aeter Twins
- **Type:** Ranged (dual pistols)
- **Personality:** Wise, deep voice. Tactician.
- **Line:** *"Keep your distance, kid. There are too many."*
- **Quirk:** Gives advice even when unasked.

### 15. Fractal Cube "Mugen"
- **Type:** Support (AoE)
- **Personality:** Arrogant, lazy. Speaks in riddles.
- **Line:** *"Missed again? Fine, I'll bend space for you..."*
- **Quirk:** Believes itself superior to all other spirits.

### 16. Scythe "Midnight"
- **Type:** Melee
- **Personality:** Mysterious. Speaks only in whispers.
- **Line:** *"...I'll take him... quietly..."*
- **Quirk:** Only appears at night.

### 17. Staff "Elder"
- **Type:** Support
- **Personality:** Wise old man. Always lecturing.
- **Line:** *"Patience, child. Strength lies in calm."*
- **Quirk:** Falls asleep mid-fight.

### 18. Axe "Berserk"
- **Type:** Melee
- **Personality:** Insane warrior. Screams, laughs.
- **Line:** *"BLOOD! PAIN! GLORY!"*

### 19. Crossbow "Sniper"
- **Type:** Ranged
- **Personality:** Cold professional. Minimal words.
- **Line:** *"Target. Shot. Done."*

### 20. Chain "Prisoner"

- **Personality:** A convict. Talks about freedom.
- **Line:** *"Freedom... someday... I'll break out..."*
- **Quirk:** Sometimes tries to "escape" from the player's hands.

### 21. Flute "Temptress"
- **Type:** Support
- **Personality:** Seductress. Hypnotizes enemies.
- **Line:** *"Come here... I don't bite... much."*
- **Quirk:** Makes enemies dance instead of fight.

### 22. Harpoon "Sailor"
- **Type:** Ranged
- **Personality:** Seasoned sailor. Speaks in sea jargon.
- **Line:** *"Yo-ho-ho! Pull that beast in!"*
- **Quirk:** Tells stories about sea monsters.

## SSR — Epic (6)

### 23. Katana "Sunset"
- **Type:** Melee
- **Personality:** Samurai. Honors the code.
- **Line:** *"Die with dignity. I will remember your name."*
- **Quirk:** Refuses to attack wounded enemies.

### 24. Twin Blades "Gemini"
- **Type:** Melee
- **Personality:** Two spirits in one weapon. Constantly arguing.
- **Line:** *"I strike first!" — "No, I do!" — "Both of you shut up!"*
- **Quirk:** Sometimes attack each other.

### 25. Bow "Moon"
- **Type:** Ranged
- **Personality:** Moon goddess. Cold, detached.
- **Line:** *"Arrows don't miss. You miss."*
- **Quirk:** Fires automatically if the player hesitates.

### 26. Hammer "Fist of God"
- **Type:** Melee
- **Personality:** God of war. Thundering voice.
- **Line:** *"KNEEL! OR BE CRUSHED!"*
- **Quirk:** Every strike causes an earthquake.

### 27. Cube "Chaos"
- **Type:** Support
- **Personality:** Insane mathematician. Speaks in formulas.
- **Line:** *"Theorem: you + me = enemy death. Proof: BOOM!"*
- **Quirk:** Changes abilities every fight.

### 28. Chain "Meditation"
- **Type:** Support
- **Personality:** Monk. Always calm.
- **Line:** *"Breathe. Find your center. Strike."*
- **Quirk:** Restores player HP in battle.

---

## UR — Legendary (2)

### 29. Blade "The First"
- **Type:** Universal (shapeshifts)
- **Personality:** The first weapon in history. Ancient as the world.
- **Line:** *"I was before the gods. I will be after. You are but a moment."*
- **Quirk:** Remembers every owner. Tells their stories.
- **Unique ability:** Can take the form of any other weapon.

### 30. Orb "The Source"
- **Type:** Universal (pure energy)
- **Personality:** The universe itself. Speaks with all voices at once.
- **Line:** *"We are all. You are part of us. Fight."*
- **Quirk:** Never says "I", only "we".
- **Unique ability:** Copies the last ultimate of a defeated boss.

## Gacha Rates

- **R:** 70%
- **SR:** 22%
- **SSR:** 7%
- **UR:** 1%

---

## Progression

Each spirit can be upgraded to level 5. At levels 3 and 5 the weapon gets visual changes (glow, runes, aura).

---

## Global Chat

When a player rolls SSR or UR, the spirit writes an epic line to the global server chat. This creates FOMO and pushes other players to roll.

---

## Hall of Ego

In the hub there is a room called the "Hall of Ego" — a collection chamber. All owned spirits float there as glowing orbs. Click one to hear its story.
